HTML Structural Hierarchy

  • The most important aspect of creating a strucutural Hierarchy is the proper use of heading elements. Heading elements are numbered h1, h2, h3 and so on.
  • Much like a text document you do not want to use heading levels in the incorrect order
  • Your h1 element is your top-level heading and you will rarely have more than one of these on a page, and it should typically come before all of your content.
  • Your h2 element is your subheading. It should always come after your h1 and might come after some introductionary text, you may have multiplier h2 elements on your page
  • Your h3 element is a subheading of your h2 element. You may have multiple h3 elements on your page, but they should always come after an h2 element.
  • Using the right Hierarchy is important for Accessibility technologies like screen readers, which rely on the structure of a webpage to determine how to parse and annouce that page to the user
  • Proper structure is also important for SEO. Search engine use automation to parse the content of your webpage and determine when and where is should show up in results
  • Using the right structure is also important for styling your webpage with CSS. You can use CSS to style your headings differently based on their level in the hierarchy
  • Using the right structure is also important for JavaScript. You can use JavaScript to manipulate the DOM and change the content of your webpage based on the structure of your headings
  • Using the right structure is also important for performance. A well-structured webpage will load faster and be more responsive to user interactions
  • Using the right structure is also important for maintainability. A well-structured webpage will be easier to read and understand, making it easier to maintain and update in the future
  • Using the right structure is also important for usability. A well-structured webpage will be easier to navigate and use, making it more user-friendly
  • Using the right structure is also important for accessibility. A well-structured webpage will be easier to use for people with disabilities, making it more inclusive
  • The h1 through h6 heading elements are used to signify the importance of content below them.
