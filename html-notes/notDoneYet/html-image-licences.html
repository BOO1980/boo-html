Image Licences

  • Images can be licensed under various terms, including Creative Commons, public domain, and commercial licenses.
  • Creative Commons licenses allow creators to specify how their work can be used, shared, and modified.
  • Public domain images are free to use without restrictions, as the creator has waived all rights.
  • Commercial licenses typically require payment or attribution for the use of the image.
  • Always check the license terms before using an image to ensure compliance with copyright laws.
  • Attribution is often required for Creative Commons images, giving credit to the original creator.
  • Some licenses may allow for modifications, while others may restrict changes to the original work.
  • Images from stock photo websites often come with specific licensing agreements that must be followed.
  • Using images without proper licensing can lead to legal issues and copyright infringement claims.
  • Many websites offer free images with various licenses, such as Unsplash, Pexels, and Pixabay.
  • When using images for commercial purposes, it's important to verify the license to avoid potential legal complications.
  • Some licenses may have specific requirements for how the image is displayed or used in a project.
